About Valley Scientific

Independent Forensic Toxicology Consulting

Eduardo Ruvalcaba, B.S.

Forensic Toxicologist & Expert Witness

Eduardo Ruvalcaba is a forensic toxicologist with more than 15 years of experience in forensic toxicology, laboratory analysis, method development, scientific data interpretation, quality assurance, and courtroom testimony.

Through Valley Scientific LLC, he provides independent forensic toxicology consulting and expert witness services to attorneys and government agencies.

His work focuses on the scientific evaluation of toxicology evidence, laboratory data, analytical procedures, method development, and the interpretation of alcohol and drug results in the context of individual cases.

Professional Credentials

Eduardo is approved by the State of California as an alcohol analyst and has extensive experience performing and evaluating forensic alcohol analysis.

His professional experience includes work with the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation (CDCR), as well as consulting on high-profile federal cases involving forensic toxicology.

Eduardo has also been admitted by the court as an expert witness in his field of forensic toxicology, including proceedings in the Superior Courts of Fresno and Tulare Counties and the United States District Court.

These experiences provide a broad perspective spanning forensic laboratory operations, analytical methods, scientific interpretation, case consultation, and courtroom testimony.

Professional Experience

With more than 15 years of experience in forensic toxicology, Eduardo's work has encompassed laboratory analysis, scientific data interpretation, method development, quality assurance, and courtroom testimony.

His professional work focuses on the development, evaluation, and implementation of forensic toxicology methods, including analytical procedures for the detection and interpretation of alcohol and drugs. This experience includes evaluating analytical performance, calibration procedures, quality controls, and the scientific reliability of laboratory methods.

Eduardo's laboratory experience provides a practical understanding of how forensic toxicology methods are developed, validated, implemented, and interpreted. This perspective allows him to evaluate not only the final toxicology result, but also the scientific and analytical processes used to produce that result.

His experience also includes reviewing laboratory procedures, analytical documentation, quality control practices, and scientific data to identify potential issues that may affect the reliability or interpretation of forensic toxicology results.

Expert Witness Experience

Eduardo has been admitted by the court as an expert witness in forensic toxicology in the Superior Courts of Fresno and Tulare Counties, as well as the United States District Court.

His courtroom experience includes providing scientific opinions and explaining complex forensic toxicology concepts in a clear and understandable manner.

Expert witness services may include deposition testimony, trial testimony, scientific consultation, review of opposing expert opinions, and assistance with the preparation and interpretation of toxicology evidence.

His laboratory background and experience with analytical methods provide an additional perspective when evaluating the scientific foundation behind forensic toxicology results presented in court.

An Independent Scientific Approach

Valley Scientific approaches every case from a scientific perspective. The goal is to examine the available evidence, identify relevant scientific issues, and provide an objective interpretation supported by the underlying data.

Complex toxicology findings can have significant consequences in litigation. Clear communication of the science is therefore an essential part of the consulting process.

Whether evaluating a reported toxicology result, reviewing laboratory analytical data, examining a laboratory method, or assessing the scientific basis of an expert opinion, the focus remains on the underlying evidence and accepted scientific principles.

Science Behind the Result

A forensic toxicology result is more than a number on a report. Understanding how that result was generated requires consideration of the analytical method, calibration, controls, instrumentation, quality assurance procedures, and the underlying scientific data.

Eduardo's experience in method development and laboratory operations provides the ability to examine these underlying components when evaluating forensic toxicology evidence.

This approach is particularly important when the reliability, interpretation, or scientific foundation of a toxicology result is at issue.
