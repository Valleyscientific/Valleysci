About Valley Scientific

Independent Forensic Toxicology Consulting

Eduardo Ruvalcaba, B.S.

Forensic Toxicologist & Expert Witness

Eduardo Ruvalcaba is a forensic toxicologist with more than 15 years of experience in forensic toxicology, laboratory analysis, scientific data interpretation, and courtroom testimony.

Through Valley Scientific LLC, he provides independent forensic toxicology consulting and expert witness services to attorneys and government agencies.

His work focuses on the scientific evaluation of toxicology evidence, laboratory data, analytical procedures, and the interpretation of alcohol and drug results in the context of individual cases.

Professional Experience

With extensive experience working within the forensic toxicology laboratory environment, Eduardo has developed a detailed understanding of the analytical process from laboratory testing through interpretation and reporting.

His experience includes forensic alcohol testing, drug analysis, laboratory quality assurance, analytical data interpretation, review of laboratory procedures, and evaluation of scientific findings.

This laboratory experience provides a practical foundation for evaluating the scientific evidence presented in criminal and civil proceedings.

Expert Witness Experience

Eduardo has been admitted by the court as an expert witness in forensic toxicology in the Superior Courts of Fresno and Tulare Counties, as well as the United States District Court.

His courtroom experience includes providing scientific opinions and explaining complex forensic toxicology concepts in a clear and understandable manner.

Expert witness services may include deposition testimony, trial testimony, scientific consultation, review of opposing expert opinions, and assistance with the preparation and interpretation of toxicology evidence.

An Independent Scientific Approach

Valley Scientific approaches every case from a scientific perspective. The goal is to examine the available evidence, identify relevant scientific issues, and provide an objective interpretation supported by the underlying data.

Complex toxicology findings can have significant consequences in litigation. Clear communication of the science is therefore an essential part of the consulting process.
